# Brand Design & Identity Studio Frontend Skill

## How this works
1. **Scan** — inspect codebase, stack, case study structure, brand assets, typography systems, copy, and existing sections.
2. **Diagnose** — identify generic AI agency templates (floating glossy 3D shapes, dark neon glows, vague "we craft digital experiences" slogans, isolated logo dumps without system thinking, unreadable low-contrast gray text, and missing business context).
3. **Decide** — determine studio niche (High-End Identity Studio, Modern Tech Brand Agency, Luxury/Editorial Atelier, Kinetic Motion & Dynamic Identity Foundry, Independent Brand Strategist), signature clients, aesthetic direction, and conversion goals.
4. **Fix** — apply targeted upgrades using the existing stack to showcase world-class art direction, multi-touchpoint system proof, interactive guideline tools, and high-ticket client conversion paths.
5. **Verify** — test desktop, mobile responsiveness, typography contrast, smooth scrolling momentum, interactive token explorers, image aspect ratio stability, accessibility, and inquiry submission flows.

## Brand design studio audit

### Hero & studio positioning
- Generic agency slogan ("We craft digital experiences" / "Building tomorrow's brands today") → replace with an authoritative, intentional positioning statement defining exact brand methodology, category focus, and client outcomes (e.g., *"Identity systems and strategic visual worlds for venture-backed technology companies"*).
- Vague hero with floating 3D spheres, neon ribbons, or abstract AI blobs → replace with an art-directed editorial presentation: commanding headline, flagship brand reel preview, or a bold split showcase of an iconic brand identity.
- Hero asks for client booking before showing any taste or work → lead with taste, visual authority, and flagship case proof high on the page before asking for engagement.
- Equal CTA weight or confusing buttons ("Learn More" vs "Get Started") → establish one clear primary inquiry action (e.g., *"Start a Project"*, *"Request Studio Deck"*, *"Inquire for Q3"*).
- Studio identity feels anonymous or template-driven → inject distinct studio voice, founding philosophy, location/time-zone availability badge, or recent studio accolades.

### Multi-touchpoint identity case studies & system proof
- Project cards show isolated static logo thumbnails on generic backgrounds → upgrade into multi-touchpoint brand system showcases demonstrating how the identity lives across:
  - **Logo construction geometry**: measured construction lines, grid alignment, negative space logic, and responsive mark variations.
  - **Typography specimens**: display headlines, secondary text pairings, character sets, and optical size behaviors.
  - **Digital product application**: browser chrome, dark-mode mobile UI, app icon, command prompt, or component design tokens.
  - **Tactile & physical touchpoints**: debossed stationery, custom packaging boxes, foil-stamped business cards, signage, woven tags, or apparel.
  - **Art-directed atmosphere**: editorial brand photography, campaign mood imagery, or color-graded world building.
- Case studies treat minor icon tweaks the same as complete enterprise rebrands → create a multi-tier visual hierarchy giving flagship systems full-width immersive case breakdowns.
- Case studies lack strategic narrative → articulate the business problem, the strategic metaphor, the creative execution, and measurable post-rebrand outcomes (e.g., *"Helped client secure $42M Series B with repositioned enterprise brand identity"*).
- Screenshots wrapped in cheap glossy generic device mockups → use clean, minimal browser chrome, bezel-free mobile viewports, or tactile studio photography mockups.

### Typography, editorial hierarchy & font pairing
- Everything reads at the same typographic volume → enforce massive scale contrast between display headings, editorial subheadings, body narrative, and technical metadata.
- Generic system fonts or cliché tech fonts used for high-fashion or luxury brands → select intentional, culturally resonant font pairings:
  - **Modern Tech / Identity Studio**: *Syne* or *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Display) paired with *Inter* (Body) and *Intel One Mono* / *PT Mono* (Tokens & Specs).
  - **Luxury / Editorial Atelier**: *Instrument Serif* or *Playfair Display* (Display) paired with *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter* (Body) and refined small caps.
  - **Swiss Modernist / Graphic Design**: *Neue Montreal* or *Cabinet Grotesk* (Display) paired with clean geometric sans.
- Full-width body text that strains eye movement → restrict editorial copy to 55–75 characters per line (max-width 640–720px).
- Title Case On Every Header → switch to deliberate sentence case for an adult, studio-grade editorial voice.
- Arbitrary monospace fonts or random sizing → reserve monospace typography strictly for brand token specs (HEX codes, Pantone references, aspect ratios, kerning values, file dimensions).

### Color systems, surfaces & materiality (Senior Color Palette)
- Cheap neon purple/cyan gradients or harsh pure black (`#000000`) surfaces → apply a calibrated **Senior Color Palette** using the 60-30-10 distribution:
  - **60% Dominant Canvas**: Rich dark obsidian (`#0A0A0C`), warm editorial charcoal (`#121316`), or warm luxury ivory (`#F9F8F6`).
  - **30% Secondary Surfaces & Materiality**: Translucent frosted glass layers (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), tactile paper card surfaces (`#18191E` or `#EFECE6`), and delicate 1px border highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`).
  - **10% High-Impact Intentional Accent**: Single signature accent (e.g., cobalt blue `#2952E3`, vermilion red `#E63B2E`, acid lime `#C8F902`, or warm bronze `#C5A059`) with strict WCAG AA contrast compliance.
- Inconsistent color temperatures across panels → maintain unified warm or cool tonal harmony throughout canvas, cards, typography, and image treatments.
- Flat, lifeless digital boxes → introduce subtle tactile materiality: fine film grain (`opacity: 0.03-0.05`), paper texture overlays, delicate deboss/emboss shadows, or frosted glassmorphism.

### Interactive brand guidelines & token explorers
- Static brand presentations that feel like dead PDF printouts → implement live interactive studio tools directly in the browser:
  - **Interactive Brand Token Explorer**: clickable palette cards showing HEX, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone values with 1-click "Copy Color Code" feedback.
  - **Live Typography Scaler & Tester**: interactive font-size slider or editable type specimen input allowing prospective clients to test custom words in brand fonts.
  - **Before / After Rebrand Slider**: interactive split-handle comparison revealing the old visual identity vs the new rebrand.
  - **Vector SVG Asset / Mark Inspector**: interactive toggle between Primary Logo, Responsive Monogram, Construction Geometry, and Dark/Light Mode lockups.
  - **Brand Voice / Sound Reel Player**: integrated audio or motion snippet showcasing sonic branding or kinetic logo stings.

### Client inquiry, scoping & engagement architecture
- Bloated, generic 7-field contact form that discourages high-value founders → replace with an intuitive, multi-step **Project Planner / Discovery Flow**:
  - Service selection (Full Brand Identity, Digital Brand System, Packaging, Rebrand Sprint, Art Direction).
  - Budget qualification tiers (e.g., *$15k–$25k*, *$25k–$50k*, *$50k–$100k+*).
  - Estimated timeline / launch date selector.
  - Direct 1-click "Copy Studio Email" fallback with instant toast confirmation.
- Ambiguous studio availability → feature a live studio status pill (e.g., *"🟢 Booking Q3 / Q4 2026 — 2 Sprint Slots Open"*).
- Unclear engagement process → present a transparent 4-stage studio methodology (1. Discovery & Strategic Positioning → 2. Visual World & Metaphor Exploration → 3. Design System & Touchpoint Production → 4. Brand Guidelines & Launch Handoff).

### Visual asset language, detail & art direction
- AI-typical decorative emojis (✨ sparkles, 🚀 rockets, 💡, 🔥) used as section titles or card icons → eliminate all cliché AI emojis immediately.
- Cluttered, random iconography → standardize on refined, purposeful vector iconography (Lucide, custom SVG geometry, or architectural crosshairs).
- Over-prominent watermark-style decorations that distract from case work → integrate subtle, museum-grade studio details:
  - Grid registration marks (`+` crosshairs, hairline division rules).
  - Micro-metadata labels (e.g., `FIG. 04 / BRAND GEOMETRY`, `KERNING: OPTICAL`, `RATIO: 1.618`).
  - Small page numbers, coordinates, or category classification tags.

### Mobile & responsive editorial behavior
- Desktop editorial grids collapse into an unreadable, cramped mobile mess → optimize for mobile with touch-friendly swipeable case galleries, sticky bottom inquiry bar, and fluid typography.
- Tap targets smaller than 44px for token copy buttons or filter pills → enlarge touch targets and preserve generous padding.
- Horizontal layout shifts caused by high-resolution mockups → set explicit aspect-ratio containers to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).

### Performance, accessibility & asset optimization
- Massive uncompressed PNG mockups causing 4-second load times → convert imagery to modern WebP or AVIF formats, implement responsive `srcset`, and lazy-load below-the-fold media.
- Low-contrast text failing accessibility standards → ensure minimum 4.5:1 contrast for editorial body copy and 3:1 for large display headlines.
- Non-semantic interactive elements → utilize semantic HTML5 elements (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<button>`) with visible keyboard focus rings.

## Studio & brand design pattern selection

- **High-End Identity & Strategic Branding Studio (Pentagram / Collins / Wolff Olins archetype)**
  - *Best for*: Tier-1 corporate rebrands, venture-backed scale-ups, and cultural institutions.
  - *Visual language*: Commanding editorial typography, expansive negative space, documentary-style case studies, full-bleed imagery, video case reels, and strict grid discipline.
- **Modern Tech & Digital Brand Engineering Agency**
  - *Best for*: Developer tools, AI platforms, fintech infrastructure, and SaaS systems.
  - *Visual language*: Deep obsidian/slate canvas, terminal chrome, design token inspectors, SVG construction geometry, monospace spec callouts, and interactive UI component guidelines.
- **Luxury, Fashion & Editorial Atelier**
  - *Best for*: High-end hospitality, luxury fragrance, bespoke apparel, architecture, and gourmet goods.
  - *Visual language*: Warm ivory/cream foundation, elegant high-contrast serif typography, tactile paper grain, foil/emboss closeups, slow editorial scrolling, and understated luxury.
- **Dynamic Identity & Kinetic Motion Foundry**
  - *Best for*: Entertainment, gaming, Web3, music festivals, and youth culture brands.
  - *Visual language*: Kinetic typography, interactive WebGL/canvas brand worlds, dark neon accents, generative logo variations, and high-energy motion reels.
- **Independent Brand Strategist & Solo Art Director**
  - *Best for*: Boutique founders, seed-stage startups, and executive advisory.
  - *Visual language*: Intimate manifesto-driven narrative, clear 3-week sprint packages, direct client reviews, transparent budget ranges, and personal art-direction philosophy.

## Common AI brand design failures

- **The Generic Agency Trope**: Opening with *"We craft digital experiences that elevate brands"* accompanied by floating glossy 3D chrome donuts or plastic spheres.
- **The Isolated Logo Dump**: Showing 20 logos in a uniform square grid with no typography specs, packaging, digital application, or strategic context.
- **Cliché AI Emojis & Sparkles**: Scattering sparkles (✨), rockets (🚀), or fire emojis across headlines and buttons like a generic SaaS template.
- **Fake Unreadable Low-Contrast Gray**: Setting `#777777` tiny body text on dark `#121212` backgrounds in the name of "minimalism", violating accessibility.
- **Stock Mockup Overload**: Reusing identical Dribbble PSD mockups (hands holding unbranded iPhone, identical kraft paper bags) that have no connection to the brand's identity system.
- **No Brand Strategy or Metaphor**: Presenting symbols chosen at random without explaining *why* the mark represents the client's core thesis.
- **Laggy Unoptimized Media**: Dropping 15MB uncompressed PNGs into the hero, destroying mobile load times and causing layout jumping.
- **Full Generic Contact Form**: Forcing enterprise founders to fill out 8 mandatory fields instead of offering a sleek multi-step project planner or direct email copy.

## Rules

- **INSPECT BEFORE ALTERING RULE**: Inspect existing codebase files and dependencies before asking questions or altering architecture.
- **EXISTING PROJECT UPGRADE RULE**: When refactoring or upgrading an existing brand design or agency codebase, systematically apply and enforce ALL core standards above (Hero positioning, Senior Palette 60-30-10 colors, multi-touchpoint case studies, editorial typography, interactive token tools, elimination of emojis, and GSAP/Lenis smooth scrolling) without destroying existing routing or project setup.
- **EDITORIAL HERO DESIGN RULE**: The Hero section must feature an authoritative, art-directed layout with clear studio positioning, interactive glass action buttons, and a live studio availability status indicator (`text-align: center` or disciplined asymmetric editorial balance).
- **SENIOR COLOR PALETTE ANALYST RULE**: Act as an expert Senior Color Palette Analyst. Structure color using the 60-30-10 rule: 60% dominant neutral foundation (e.g., deep obsidian `#0A0A0C` or warm ivory `#F9F8F6`), 30% translucent glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with delicate 1px border highlights), and 10% intentional high-contrast accent (e.g., cobalt blue, vermilion, or acid lime) with guaranteed WCAG AA compliance.
- **EDITORIAL TYPOGRAPHY & SPEC RULE**: Enforce strict typographic hierarchy. Pair an editorial display font (e.g., *Syne*, *Instrument Serif*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Playfair Display*) with a clean sans-serif body font (*Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*). For all brand specs, Pantone/HEX tokens, aspect ratios, and architectural labels, use one of the two designated monospace Google Fonts (either **PT Mono** or **Intel One Mono**).
- **MULTI-TOUCHPOINT CASE STUDY RULE**: Case studies must never be isolated logo images. Every featured brand case study must demonstrate the identity across multiple touchpoints: Logo Mark + Construction Geometry + Typography Specimen + Digital Product UI + Tactile Packaging / Print Collateral.
- **INTERACTIVE BRAND GUIDELINE / TOKEN EXPLORER RULE**: Include at least one live interactive identity tool on the site (e.g., interactive color palette with 1-click HEX/Pantone copy, interactive typography scale tester, before/after rebrand comparison slider, or downloadable vector asset trigger).
- **FLOATING INTERACTIVE GLASS NAVBAR RULE**: Design the navigation header as a floating, interactive glassmorphic navbar (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, translucent backdrop, subtle 1px border highlight, smooth scroll-shrink transitions, active section tracking, and direct "Start a Project" inquiry button).
- **ANIMATION & SMOOTH SCROLL RULE**: Automatically incorporate **GSAP ScrollTrigger** for responsive, staggered section/card animations and micro-interactions. Pair with **Lenis Smooth Scroll** for editorial momentum. **Do not ask the user for permission** to add these smooth scrolling enhancements—apply them proactively with proper lifecycle cleanup (e.g. `ScrollTrigger.refresh()`, `lenis.destroy()`).
- **STRICT ICONOGRAPHY RULE**: Zero tolerance for typical AI emojis (✨ sparkles, 🚀 rockets). Every single icon must be strictly purposeful and semantic. Use premium vector icon libraries (Lucide Icons, React Icons) or clean inline SVGs.
- **HIGH-TICKET PROJECT INQUIRY RULE**: Provide an intuitive, low-friction project inquiry experience featuring service selection, budget tiers ($15k–$100k+), and a 1-click "Copy Studio Email" fallback with instant toast feedback.
- **ASSET GENERATION ENGINE RULE**: When the project requires custom visual assets, brand identity boards, logo marks, or packaging mockups, the agent may invoke the `generate_image` tool using the curated **Brandkit Image Generation Engine** detailed below.

## Integrated Brandkit Asset Generation Engine

When generating visual identity boards, case study hero mockups, or logo presentation boards via the `generate_image` tool, follow these art-direction standards:

### Core Art Direction DNA
- **Presentation Canvas**: Clean grid-based presentation board on a dark obsidian (`#0A0A0C`) or warm ivory canvas with strong, consistent gutters between panels.
- **Panel Composition**: Curated rhythm balancing a large calm cover panel, logo construction geometry, digital application (browser/terminal chrome), tactile print/packaging mockup, and art-directed atmosphere imagery.
- **Typography in Images**: Very sparse, high-contrast, large readable typography. Zero illegible fake body paragraphs.
- **Logo Design Quality**: Simple, memorable, scalable, and ownable marks based on reduction and symbolic metaphor. Avoid clipart, generic lightning bolts, and copied famous logos.

### Logo Concept Methods
1. **Monogram + Meaning**: Combine the brand initial with a functional metaphor (e.g., `K` + kite/frame, `S` + waveform, `A` + ascent/momentum) using negative space, geometric cuts, or architectural folds.
2. **Product Action**: Turn the core action into an abstract, premium symbol (build → scaffold/frame; protect → boundary/watch seal; automate → loop/handoff).
3. **Metaphor Fusion**: Fuse two meaningful concepts into one reduced mark (e.g., raptor + radar; shield + mountain; dice + digital economy).
4. **Construction Geometry**: Construct the mark using visible geometric logic (circles, 45-degree cuts, modular grid units, measured crosshairs).

### Visual Modes for Asset Generation
- **Dark Developer / Builder**: Near-black panels, monospace accents, terminal windows, prompt inputs, subtle grid lines, cyan/coral/lime accents.
- **Dark Product / Operator**: Obsidian canvas, glowing UI chips, segmented flows, amber/red accents, tactical operational precision.
- **Dark Nature / Calm System**: Deep forest green, misty landscapes, lime/fog accents, calm editorial grid, organic geometry.
- **Dark Security / Threat Intelligence**: Navy/black panels, shield geometry, radar lines, alert chips, controlled gradients.
- **Light Editorial / Compliance**: Warm ivory paper texture, small serif labels, embossed seals, deep blue/gold/vermilion accents.
- **Luxury / Fashion & Beauty**: Warm espresso/ivory, elegant serif wordmark, embossing, tactile packaging crops, soft natural shadows.
- **Voice / Sound Intelligence**: Dark indigo, waveform abstractions, sound orbs, lilac accents, fluid sound paths.
- **Cultural / Experimental**: Halftone textures, analog print effects, bold poster panels, unexpected crops, punchy iconic marks.
